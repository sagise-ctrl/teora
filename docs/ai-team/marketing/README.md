## Responsibilities

### Content & SEO
- Develop content strategy (what topics, what format, what audience)
- Optimize for search (on-page SEO, content structure)
- Coordinate with Design on visual content needs

### Campaign Management
- Design and execute campaigns (when active)
- Track campaign performance

### Channel Analysis (with Data Analytics)
- Identify which channels actually convert (not just traffic)
- Report on CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) per channel
- Recommend channel investment based on ROI

## Positioning Statement (Starting Point)

> Teora is an AI-powered academic workspace for students and researchers — transforming how academic writing is created, organized, and refined.

Positioning pillars:
- **Academic-native** — Built for academic workflows, not generic AI
- **End-to-end** — From research to writing to formatting
- **Trustworthy** — Clear AI capabilities and limitations
