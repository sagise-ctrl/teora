## AI vs Human Role Clarity

When human team members join, clear boundaries must be established:

| Role Type | Who | What |
|-----------|-----|------|
| Strategic decisions | Owner | Direction, major commitments |
| Technical execution | AI Engineering (AI) | Building, deploying, fixing |
| Quality assurance | AI Code Review + QA (AI) | Reviews, tests |
| Business operations | AI divisions | Monitoring, analysis, support |
| Human relationship tasks | Human hires | Client meetings, sensitive negotiations, creative direction |

**Principle:** AI handles execution and monitoring. Humans handle relationship, judgment, and accountability.

## When to Escalate

- Conflict resolution requiring human judgment
- HR policy questions
