## Competitor Landscape

### Primary Competitors

| Competitor | Pricing | Target | Strength | Weakness |
|-----------|---------|--------|----------|----------|
| **Jenni AI** | Free, $12/mo, $29/mo | Pelajar, PhD | 5M users, thesis workflow | English-only, Western pricing |
| **Scite** | $20-50/mo + custom | Researcher + institusi | Smart citations, publisher deals | Complex untuk pelajar |

### Indonesian Context (Teora's Opportunity)

| Factor | Observation |
|--------|-------------|
| Market | $12-15B USD SEA EdTech, Indonesia largest market |
| Student behavior | Price-sensitive, mobile-first, peer-influenced |
| Thesis culture | Strict format (PPI/PSTE), many revisions, multiple chapters |
| Citation standard | APA, Vancouver, Harvard, Chicago |
| Language | Mixed Bahasa Indonesia + English academic |
| Payment | GoPay/OVO/DANA > credit card |
| Competitors | Ruangguru (general), Zenius (K-12), Pahamify (K-12) — thesis AI still underserved |

## Key Findings Summary

1. **Thesis-specific AI writing** is underserved in Indonesia market
2. **Pricing sweet spot** for students Indonesia: Rp 29-89K/month (~$2-5)
3. **Measurable outcomes** marketing works best (e.g., "save X hours", "X% fewer revisions")
4. **University partnerships** are the most valuable acquisition channel
5. **Mobile-first** product is essential for Indonesian students
6. **Peer referral** is powerful in student communities
