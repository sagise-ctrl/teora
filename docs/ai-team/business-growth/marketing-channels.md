# Marketing Channels — Teora

## Channel Overview

Not all marketing channels are equal for Teora's audience and positioning. This document evaluates channels by effectiveness, cost, and fit.

## Evaluated Channels

### High Priority Channels

#### 1. Word-of-Mouth / Peer Referral ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Effectiveness | Highest for student market |
| Cost | Very low (referral incentives) |
| Why | Students trust peers over ads |
| Action | Build referral program into product |

**Examples from competitors:**
- Jenni AI: "Share with a friend, both get credits"
- Canva: "Invite 5 friends = Pro free 1 year"

**Recommendation:** Teora referral program:
- Referrer: +1,000 free tokens
- Referee: 50% off first month
- Cap: 3 referrals max per user (prevent abuse)

---

#### 2. University Ambassador Program ⭐⭐⭐⭐

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Effectiveness | High — campus-based influence |
| Cost | Medium (ambassador perks + some budget) |
| Why | Student communities are campus-based |
| Channels | Line/WhatsApp groups, campus events |

**Examples:**
- Ruangguru uses campus ambassadors
- TikTok Tokopedia Academy ambassadors

**Recommendation:** Ambassadors get:
- Free Pro account
- Commission on referred subscribers (5-10%)
- Exclusive early access to new features

---

#### 3. Content Marketing (Blog / Tutorial) ⭐⭐⭐⭐

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Effectiveness | Medium-high, long-term |
| Cost | Low (can do in-house) |
| Why | Students search "cara menulis skripsi", "cara buat sitasi" |
| SEO value | High — educational content = evergreen |

**Content topics:**
- "Cara bikin outline skripsi dari 0"
- "Format sitasi APA 7th edition lengkap"
- "Tutorial EndNote vs Zotero vs Teora"
- "10 kesalahan umum saat menulis skripsi"

**Recommendation:** Publish 2 blog posts/week. Focus on Google Search (not social) for evergreen traffic.

---

#### 4. YouTube Tutorials ⭐⭐⭐⭐

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Effectiveness | High — students learn via video |
| Cost | Medium (video production) |
| Why | "How to use" content converts well |
| Potential | Product walkthrough, tips & tricks |

**Recommendation:** Start with simple screen recordings (Loom/Obs). Upgrade to edited videos as channel grows.

---

### Medium Priority Channels

#### 5. Instagram Reels / TikTok ⭐⭐⭐

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Effectiveness | High reach, low conversion |
| Cost | Low-medium |
| Why | Viral potential, student audience |
| Content | Short tips: "3 menit bikin outline skripsi" |

**Note:** Good for awareness, harder to convert to paid. Use as top-of-funnel.

---

#### 6. WhatsApp / Line Groups ⭐⭐⭐

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Effectiveness | High in Indonesia (Line dominant) |
| Cost | Very low |
| Why | Primary communication for students |
| Approach | Community groups, not broadcast spam |

**Recommendation:** Create themed Line groups ("Skripsi Survivors Indonesia") as community hub, not direct marketing.

---

#### 7. SEO / Google Ads ⭐⭐

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Effectiveness | High if targeting right keywords |
| Cost | Medium-high ($1-5 CPC for EdTech) |
| Why | Intent-based traffic converts |
| Keywords | "AI writing skripsi", "bikin skripsi cepat", "sitasi generator" |

**Note:** Competitive keywords are expensive. Start with long-tail, low-CPC keywords.

---

### Low Priority / Future

#### 8. Podcast / Blog Features ⭐⭐

- EduTuber collaborations
- Student podcast features
- EdTech blog mentions

**Cost:** Low but requires outreach effort.

---

#### 9. University Partnership / Institutional Outreach ⭐⭐

- Partner with thesis guidance centers
- Offer free trial to thesis consulting services
- Academic conference sponsorships

**Note:** This is B2B channel, relevant for Option B/C pricing.

---

## Channel Strategy by Phase

| Phase | Primary Channels | Secondary |
|-------|-----------------|-----------|
| Pre-launch | Content marketing (SEO), waitlist | Social media |
| Launch (0-3 mo) | Referral program, content, YouTube | Instagram/TikTok |
| Growth (3-6 mo) | Add university ambassadors | Line/WhatsApp communities |
| Scale (6-12 mo) | University partnerships (B2B) | Google Ads (if budget) |

---

## Budget Allocation Recommendation

Assuming Rp 5-10 juta/month marketing budget:

| Channel | Allocation | Amount |
|---------|-----------|--------|
| Referral program (credits) | 30% | Rp 1.5-3M value |
| Content (blog + YouTube) | 30% | Rp 1.5-3M |
| Ambassador program | 20% | Rp 1-2M |
| Social media (organic) | 10% | Rp 500K-1M |
| Contingency / experiments | 10% | Rp 500K-1M |

---

## What NOT to Spend On

- **Paid ads (early stage):** Too expensive, no brand recognition yet
- **Influencer marketing:** Expensive, low ROI for EdTech
- **Conference booths:** Too broad, low conversion
- **PR agencies:** Too expensive for early-stage
