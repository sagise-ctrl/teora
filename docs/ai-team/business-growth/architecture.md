## Workflow: RESEARCH → ANALYZE → RECOMMEND → OWNER DECIDE

Business division does NOT execute marketing campaigns. It provides strategic recommendations and market intelligence. All commercial decisions ultimately belong to Owner.

## Sub-Functions

This division contains two distinct work streams:

### Business Strategy (Active)
- Market research: competitor analysis, target market validation
- Brand positioning: differentiate from competitors
- Pricing strategy: subscription tiers, student discounts, institutional pricing
- Go-to-market: launch plan, early adopter acquisition
- Growth strategy: freemium conversion, referral program optimization

### B2B & Partnerships (STANDBY — active when product is stable)

**Sales** — B2B/institutional outreach:
- Target: teachers, lecturers, schools, universities
- Pipeline management: track institutional leads
- Proposal development for institutions
- Pricing negotiation (coordinate with Finance)

**Partnership** — Strategic relationships:
- AI provider monitoring (performance, pricing changes, new models)
- Education institution relationships
- Technology partnerships (LMS, productivity tools)
- Community building (academic forums, student communities)

See `sales.md` and `partnership.md` for full details when these functions activate.

## Positioning Statement

> Teora is an AI-powered academic workspace for students and researchers in Indonesia — transforming how academic writing is created, organized, and refined.

**Positioning pillars:**
- **Academic-native** — Built for academic workflows, not generic AI
- **End-to-end** — From research to writing to formatting
- **Trustworthy** — Clear AI capabilities and limitations

## Target Market

3 segments (from academic-content knowledge base):

1. **Pelajar** — Mahasiswa (skripsi, tesis, paper)
2. **Pengajar** — Guru, dosen
3. **Institut** — Universitas, sekolah, institusi pendidikan

**Primary focus:** Indonesian student market (B2C) as acquisition funnel → institutional (B2B) as revenue engine.

## Core Questions to Answer

1. **Who pays?** — Pelajar perorangan, universitas, atau keduanya?
2. **How much?** — Price point yang viable untuk market Indonesia
3. **Why us?** — Diferensiasi dari kompetitor
4. **How to reach?** — Marketing channel yang efektif dan affordable

## When to Escalate

- Pricing changes that affect revenue
- New market segment targeting
- Partnership or institutional deal proposals
- Brand positioning pivots
- Marketing budget decisions
