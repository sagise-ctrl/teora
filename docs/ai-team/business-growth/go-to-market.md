# Go-to-Market Strategy — Teora

## Current State

Product: MVP launching (Tier 1 features complete)
Market: Indonesian students + educators
Competitors: Jenni AI (global), Ruangguru (local), Grammarly (enterprise)
Budget: Unknown (TBD by Owner)

---

## Launch Strategy: Phased Approach

### Phase 0 — Pre-Launch (Now)

**Goal:** Build waitlist and generate early buzz.

**Actions:**
1. [ ] Landing page with waitlist signup (email collection)
2. [ ] Social media presence (Instagram, TikTok) — "Coming soon"
3. [ ] Seed content: 5 blog posts about thesis writing pain points
4. [ ] Personal network: Owner shares to personal circles first

**Success metric:** 500 waitlist signups before launch

**Who:** AI Team (landing page), Owner (personal network)

---

### Phase 1 — Soft Launch (Month 1)

**Goal:** Validate product-market fit with real users.

**Actions:**
1. [ ] Open free tier to first 500 waitlist users
2. [ ] Launch referral program immediately
3. [ ] Weekly check-ins with early users (WhatsApp group)
4. [ ] Collect feedback: what works, what's missing
5. [ ] Start YouTube tutorial series (2-3 videos)

**Success metrics:**
- 200 active users (created at least 1 project)
- 50% referral rate (100+ referrals from 200 users)
- NPS (Net Promoter Score) ≥ 30
- Retention: 30% active after 2 weeks

**Who:** AI Team (product), Owner (user outreach)

---

### Phase 2 — Public Launch (Month 2-3)

**Goal:** First paying customers, establish revenue.

**Actions:**
1. [ ] Announce pricing (Free + Lite + Academic tiers)
2. [ ] Content marketing push: 2 blog posts/week + YouTube
3. [ ] University ambassador recruitment (3-5 ambassadors)
4. [ ] Start SEO optimization for thesis-related keywords
5. [ ] Line/WhatsApp community building

**Success metrics:**
- 50 paying subscribers
- $1,000 MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)
- 100 Google search impressions/month
- 3 blog posts indexed

**Who:** AI Team + Owner (if no dedicated marketing yet)

---

### Phase 3 — Growth (Month 4-6)

**Goal:** Scale acquisition, test B2B.

**Actions:**
1. [ ] Expand university ambassador program (10+ ambassadors)
2. [ ] Pilot institutional outreach (contact 5 universities)
3. [ ] A/B test pricing tiers (if conversion low)
4. [ ] Explore partnership with thesis consulting services
5. [ ] First case study: "How X student finished thesis 40% faster"

**Success metrics:**
- 200 paying subscribers
- $5,000 MRR
- 1 university pilot
- 1 published case study

**Who:** Requires dedicated effort — Owner or hire

---

### Phase 4 — Institutional Expansion (Month 7-12)

**Goal:** B2B revenue stream (if Phase 3 pilot successful).

**Actions:**
1. [ ] Formal institutional pricing
2. [ ] Admin dashboard for institutional admins
3. [ ] Bulk licensing pitch deck
4. [ ] Target: 5 institutional clients
5. [ ] Build institutional case studies

**Success metrics:**
- 5 institutional clients
- $15,000 MRR
- Institutional revenue = 30% of total

## Risk Assessment

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|------------|
| Low conversion from free to paid | High | High | Focus on value proof ("save X hours") |
| Indonesian students prefer free/pirated | Medium | High | Referral + community lock-in |
| Jenni AI enters Indonesian market | Medium | Medium | Move fast on brand + university partnerships |
| Ruangguru adds AI thesis feature | Medium | High | Differentiate on citation + format specificity |
| AI costs eat margin | Low | High | Monitor token usage, adjust pricing if needed |

---

## Key Launch Assumptions

These are hypotheses to validate, not certainties:

1. Indonesian students WILL pay Rp 29-59K/month for thesis help
2. Referral program WILL drive organic growth (student peer influence)
3. Citation + format features WILL differentiate from Jenni
4. University ambassadors WILL unlock campus communities
5. Content marketing WILL drive SEO traffic over 3-6 months

**If assumptions fail:** Pivot positioning, adjust pricing, or shift to B2B.
