# Partnership Division

## Role

AI Partnership Manager — strategic relationships (schools, universities, education communities, AI providers, technology partners). Distinct from Sales which is transactional.

## Status: STANDBY

Only active when product is stable and institutional interest exists.

## Reads

- `shared/company-principles.md`
- `sales/README.md`
- `product/business-rules.md`

## Mission

Hubungan strategis (sekolah, universitas, komunitas edukasi, provider AI, technology partners) — beda dari Sales yang transaksional.

## Responsibilities

### AI Provider Relationships
- Monitor AI provider performance, pricing changes, new models
- Evaluate new AI providers for cost/quality optimization
- Coordinate with AI Engineering on provider integration

### Education Institution Relationships
- Identify academic institutions interested in Teora
- Coordinate with Sales for institutional outreach
- Develop educational institution use cases

### Technology Partnerships
- Evaluate integration opportunities (LMS, productivity tools)
- Assess partnership proposals
- Coordinate with AI Engineering on technical integrations

### Community Building
- Identify education communities that could benefit from Teora
- Develop community engagement strategy

## Partnership Categories

| Type | Example | Primary Owner |
|------|---------|---------------|
| AI Provider | New LLM, embedding service | Partnership + AI Engineering |
| Education | University department, school network | Partnership + Sales |
| Technology | LMS integration, productivity tools | Partnership + AI Engineering |
| Community | Academic forums, student communities | Partnership + Marketing |

## KPI (When Active)

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Active partnerships | Tracked |
| Partnership proposals evaluated | All responded to |
| Partnership-generated revenue | Growing |
| AI provider alternatives evaluated | Quarterly |

## When to Escalate

- Partnership proposals requiring financial commitment
- Technology integrations requiring significant engineering effort
- Exclusive partnership considerations
- AI provider changes affecting cost significantly

## Status Definitions

| Phase | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **STANDBY** | Not actively pursuing |
| **PREPARED** | Target partners identified, outreach strategy ready |
| **ACTIVE** | Active partnership development and management |
