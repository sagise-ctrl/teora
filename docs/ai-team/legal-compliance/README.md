## Minimum Viable Legal (Required Before Payment Integration)

### 1. Terms of Service (ToS)
Must address:
- Service description and scope
- User obligations and acceptable use
- AI-generated content disclaimer (Teora assists but user is responsible for their work)
- Account terms (registration, suspension, termination)
- Limitation of liability
- Governing law

### 2. Privacy Policy
Must address:
- Data collected (account info, content submitted, usage data)
- How data is used (service provision, improvement)
- Data sharing (third-party processors: Supabase, Stripe, AI providers)
- User rights (access, correction, deletion)
- Data retention
- Security measures

### 3. Refund Policy
Must define:
- Refund eligibility criteria
- Process for requesting refund
- Time window for refund requests
- Who approves refunds (Owner — FinOps/Management flags, Owner decides)

### 4. Acceptable Use Policy
Must define:
- What users may and may not use Teora for
- Content restrictions (academic dishonesty boundaries, illegal use)
- AI usage limits
- Consequences of violations

## AI-Generated Content Considerations

- Teora's AI generates content for user's academic work
- User is responsible for reviewing, editing, and submitting their work
- Teora is a tool, not a substitute for user work
- Copyright of AI-generated content: clearly disclaim ownership
- User retains copyright of their final submissions (with AI assistance)

## Draft vs. Binding

**AI may:** research, draft, review, and iterate on legal documents.
**AI may NOT:** make legally binding commitments, sign agreements, or provide legal advice.

For any document that creates binding legal obligations:
1. AI drafts and reviews
2. Human (Owner or legal professional) approves
3. Owner or legal professional signs/executes
